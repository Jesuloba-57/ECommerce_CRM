Project direction

From static storefront to negotiation-first marketplace

The original concept described a Facebook Marketplace-style experience where buyers and sellers interact directly, sellers can react quickly to demand, and every product keeps a visible history of price changes. This refresh turns that idea into a working app structure instead of a brochure site.

What matters most

Marketplace behaviors

Direct buyer-seller interaction

Offers are now part of the product flow, so buyers can negotiate instead of only accepting a fixed price.

Fast price updates for sellers

Sellers can adjust listing prices from a dashboard and keep a reason attached to each change.

Transparent price history

Each listing records its pricing timeline to build buyer trust and show how demand changes over time.
